Rules

How this project works

Durak

Attack with cards. Defend with higher same suit or trump. If you cannot defend, take the table. Cheat Peek can reveal enemy cards, but one trap card disables cheating.

Chess

Move pieces by normal movement rules. Pawns promote to queens. Capturing the king wins in this arcade version.

Checkers

Move diagonally, capture enemy pieces, and promote to kings on the last row. Captures are forced when available.

Online

Create a room and share the invite link. For real online play, run this project on a Node.js host such as Render or Railway.

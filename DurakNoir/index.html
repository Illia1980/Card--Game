How to play

Durak Rules

1. Attack

Attacker places a card on the table. Extra attack cards must match a rank already on the table.

2. Defend

Defender must beat the attack card with a higher card of the same suit or with a trump card.

3. Trump

The trump suit is shown near the deck. Any trump beats any non-trump card.

4. Beat or Take

If all attacks are defended, press Beat. If you cannot defend, press Take and collect the table.

5. Goal

Get rid of all your cards after the deck is empty. The player left with cards is the Durak.

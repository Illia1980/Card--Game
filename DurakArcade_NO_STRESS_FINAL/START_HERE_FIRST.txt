START HERE FIRST

LOCAL TEST ON YOUR COMPUTER
1. Double click START_GAME.bat
2. Wait until the black server window opens
3. Browser opens http://localhost:3000
4. Press Play vs Bot or Play with Friend

IMPORTANT
Do not open public/index.html directly.
Do not use localhost:63342 for multiplayer.
Use only http://localhost:3000 when testing locally.

TO PLAY WITH FRIENDS ONLINE
1. Upload this whole folder to GitHub.
2. Deploy the GitHub repository on Render.
3. Render gives you a public link.
4. Open that link, create a room, copy invite link, send it to a friend.

Read DEPLOY_RENDER_SIMPLE.md for the exact online steps.
